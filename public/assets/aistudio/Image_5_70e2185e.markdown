## Brand & Style

This design system is built for a professional government and institutional compliance platform. The target audience includes compliance officers, legal auditors, and public sector administrators who require absolute clarity, efficiency, and zero ambiguity. The UI evokes a sense of trust, institutional authority, and rigorous precision through a clean, corporate-modern design aesthetic. Every visual element is crafted to reduce cognitive load during complex regulatory reviews and data-dense multi-state audits.

## Colors

The color palette centers on clean light-neutral backgrounds (`#F8FAFC`) paired with dark navy headings (`#0F172A`) for optimal reading contrast. Authoritative royal blue (`#0284C7`) and deep teal (`#0D9488`) serve as primary and secondary accents to highlight actionable controls and active compliance states. Semantic indicators must adhere strictly to WCAG AAA contrast ratios for status messaging, error states, and institutional data visualizations.

## Typography

Inter is utilized across all weights to provide a utilitarian, highly legible typographic hierarchy optimized for dense data tables and legal text. Font sizes are tightly controlled to maximize information density without sacrificing readability on standard desktop monitors and enterprise terminals. Use semibold weights exclusively for primary data points, table headers, and section titles.

## Layout & Spacing

The platform uses a structural, fixed-max-width grid system optimized for desktop-first compliance workflows and data-heavy dashboards. A predictable 12-column layout with 24px gutters ensures consistent alignment of audit logs, telemetry panels, and multi-step reporting wizards. Spacing increments strictly follow an 8px baseline grid to maintain visual rhythm across dense analytical views.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is established primarily through low-contrast outlines ("ghost borders") and precise surface-container tonal shifts rather than dramatic shadows. This restrained approach prevents visual clutter in dense data environments. Interactive elements such as dropdowns and modal dialogs employ subtle, crisp borders combined with restrained ambient shadows to denote elevation cleanly and professionally.

## Shapes

The shape language employs a conservative, highly functional soft style (`roundedness` level 1). Interactive components feature subtle 0.25rem rounded corners, while major structural containers and cards utilize 0.5rem radii. This slight rounding softens the institutional density of the platform while preserving a sharp, authoritative posture.

## Components

### Buttons
Primary actions use the authoritative royal blue (`#0284C7`) with white text and a subtle border radius. Secondary actions feature clean ghost outlines with dark navy labels. Destructive actions must incorporate high-contrast red fills with clear focus rings.

### Chips & Status Badges
Status indicators must use high-contrast pill designs with distinct background tints and dark text to clearly communicate compliance states (e.g., Verified, Flagged, Pending Review, Non-Compliant). 

### Data Tables
Tables are the core component of this platform. They must feature compact row heights, sticky headers, subtle horizontal dividers, tabular numerals for numerical data, and persistent inline pagination controls.

### Form Elements & Inputs
Inputs require distinct, persistent border outlines, clear floating or top-aligned labels, and accessible focus states. Checkboxes and radio buttons must maintain high-contrast borders with unambiguous checked indicators.

### Cards & Containers
Information is organized within structured metric cards and collapsible accordion panels that partition complex compliance domains into digestible segments.
